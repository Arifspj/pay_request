# Privacy Policy — Pay Request

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Data Collection
Pay Request does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data created within the app (payment requests, favorites, settings) is stored locally on your device using SQLite.

## Permissions
The app requests the following permissions:

- **Camera** — Used exclusively for scanning UPI QR codes. No images or video are recorded or transmitted.
- **Contacts** — Used to let you select contacts from your phone book when creating payment requests. Contact data is never sent off-device.
- **Internet** — Used solely to open external links (WhatsApp `wa.me` URLs, Play Store link for rating). No analytics or tracking.

## Third-Party Services
When you share a payment request via WhatsApp or other apps, the content you choose to share is handled entirely by that third-party service. Pay Request has no control over how those services process your data.

## Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally, deleting the app removes all associated data. You can also export and delete your history manually from within the Settings screen.

## Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: privacy@pay-request.app
