Pay Request makes it easy to send and collect UPI payments from anyone. Whether you're a small business owner, freelancer, or just splitting bills with friends, Pay Request simplifies the entire process.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Scan any UPI QR code to auto-fill merchant details
2. Enter the amount and optionally attach an invoice/receipt image
3. Select a contact from favorites or your phone book
4. Share the payment request directly via WhatsApp — message + QR code included

KEY FEATURES
• UPI QR Scanner — Scan any UPI-compatible QR code to instantly capture merchant name and UPI ID
• Payment Requests — Create requests with amount, invoice attachment, and contact details
• WhatsApp Integration — Share requests directly to any WhatsApp contact with pre-filled payment message
• QR Code Generation — Every request includes a scannable QR code for quick UPI payments
• Favorites — Save frequently-used contacts for one-tap access
• Payment History — Track all your requests with status indicators (Pending, Shared, Completed)
• Export History — Export all transactions as a text file for record-keeping
• Dark Mode — Comfortable viewing in any lighting condition
• Invoice Attachment — Capture or pick images to attach to payment requests
• No Account Required — Works immediately, no sign-up needed

PERFECT FOR
• Small business owners collecting payments
• Freelancers sending invoices
• Friends splitting group expenses
• Landlords collecting rent
• Anyone who needs to request UPI payments

Privacy-first: All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking.

Note: Requires WhatsApp to be installed for direct sharing. Camera permission needed for QR scanning. Contact permission needed for accessing phone book.
