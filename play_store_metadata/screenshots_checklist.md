# Screenshots Checklist — Google Play Store

## Required (2-8 screenshots)

| # | Screen | Content | Text Overlay |
|---|--------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | Home Screen | Hero banner, recent requests, quick actions | "Send UPI Payment Requests Instantly" |
| 2 | Scan QR | Camera viewfinder scanning a UPI QR code | "Scan any UPI QR code" |
| 3 | Create Request | Form with merchant, amount, invoice fields | "Add amount & invoice" |
| 4 | Share via WhatsApp | System share sheet or WhatsApp chat preview | "Share directly on WhatsApp" |
| 5 | Favorites | Favorite contacts list | "Save frequent contacts" |
| 6 | Settings | Dark mode, export, share app options | "Customize your experience" |

## Specifications
- **Size:** 1080 x 1920 px (or 1080 x 2340 px for taller devices)
- **Format:** PNG (lossless)
- **Max size:** 8 MB per screenshot
- **Orientation:** Portrait preferred
- **No status bar** — use a clean device frame

## Feature Graphic (required)
- **Size:** 1024 x 500 px
- **Format:** PNG or JPG
- **Content:** App name + tagline on a branded background
- **Text overlay:** "Pay Request — UPI Payment Requests Made Easy"

## Tips
- Use the light theme for screenshots
- Use realistic sample data (not "123", "Test")
- Keep text overlays short and readable
- Avoid covering key UI elements with overlays
